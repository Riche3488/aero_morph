    Apply to full-resolution geometry:
    python3 2_shape_morphing/drag_morph.py --in body.stl --mode my_mode.json=1.0 --out out --render
    Load an STL (left panel or drag & drop)
    Drag handle: press on the surface and drag.
    Anchor: click to pin a point (it does not move).
    Click a handle sphere to select it or re-drag it. Right-drag or Orbit mode rotates, wheel zooms.
    Shift while dragging = along surface normal.
