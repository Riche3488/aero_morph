Drag a handle or the car surface · help ▾
Grab a coloured handle and drag it along its arrow, or press anywhere on the car and drag: the point follows the mouse through the morph field. The drag direction picks the variable that moves that point that way, e.g.
nose forward/back → front overhang · tail → rear overhang
rear half / rear wheel along x → length · side panel sideways → width
roof end / spoiler up-down → spoiler height
Shift = 10 mm steps · Ctrl+Z undo
Background drag or right-drag = rotate, middle-drag = pan, wheel = zoom.
